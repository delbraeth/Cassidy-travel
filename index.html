About

Patrick Cassidy

I'm Patrick Cassidy, an independent travel advisor in Waynesville, Ohio. I plan cruises, resort stays, theme park trips and family getaways. My specialty is the trips with more moving parts: three generations on one vacation, families with young kids, teams on the road, and travelers who need an accessible room or a calmer pace.

I spent years directing US Paralympic swim meets, and I travel with a power wheelchair myself. So I ask the questions a brochure skips, then I confirm the answers before you book.
