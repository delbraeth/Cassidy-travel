Disney Cruise Line

$$$$

Polished, theatrical and built around families, with characters, Broadway-style shows and the best kids' clubs at sea.

Best for
Families with kids of any age, Disney fans, a first cruise that has to go right
Kids and teens
Separate clubs for kids, tweens and teens, plus a nursery for babies (extra cost)
Dining
Rotational dining: a different themed restaurant each night, with the same servers following you
Included
Main dining, buffet, soft drinks and most room service. Alcohol and Wi-Fi extra; tips added

Bahamas and Caribbean, Alaska, Europe, Mexico, Hawaii, Australia, Singapore

Spotlight

Alaska: which line?

Almost every line sails to Alaska, so the choice comes down to how much of Alaska you want to see and who's coming.

  • Cruise only or cruise plus land? A cruisetour adds a few nights inland, usually Denali. Princess and Holland America run the most complete cruisetours, with their own lodges and rail cars.
  • Round trip or one way? Round trips from Seattle or Vancouver stick to the Inside Passage. One-way trips across the Gulf of Alaska reach more glaciers and connect to a land tour.
  • Glacier Bay? The National Park Service limits how many ships enter each day. Princess and Holland America have the most visits; Norwegian and Viking have some. Most other lines visit Hubbard Glacier or a fjord such as Endicott Arm instead.
  • When to go: the season runs May to September. Book 12 to 18 months out for the best cabins, and a balcony is worth it here.
