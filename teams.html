Teams and clubs

Get the whole team to the meet, and home again.

Swim meets, tournaments and club trips, planned from Waynesville, Ohio. I've served as meet director for the Bill Keating, Jr. Memorial Cincinnati Para-Swimming Open, so I've seen team travel from the event side too. I travel with a power wheelchair myself, so I check the details adaptive teams depend on.

Start a team trip (opens in a new tab)

"Start a team trip" opens a short form on Fora Travel, my host agency, in a new tab. In the notes, include the event, dates, venue, headcount, and how many travelers use wheelchairs.

What does it cost? I don't charge a planning fee.

What I handle

  • A room block near the venue. I count the accessible rooms your team needs before the contract is signed. Many hotels have only a few, and they're the first to run out.
  • The rooming list. Who sleeps where, arrival and departure dates, and the changes as your roster changes.
  • Getting there. Transport for people and gear, by road or air.
  • One contact for parents and coaches. Questions come to me instead of five different websites.

What to have ready

You don't need all of it to start, but these get you a plan faster.

  • The event name, dates and venue address
  • Headcount: athletes, coaches, staff and family
  • How many travelers use wheelchairs or other mobility devices, and any other access needs
  • Who's paying: the club, each family, or a mix
  • Your budget, if you have one

Common questions

Do you only plan adaptive sports travel?

No. I plan for all kinds of teams and clubs. Adaptive teams are where my experience helps most.

How far ahead should we start?

As soon as you know the meet dates. Hotels near popular venues fill early, and the accessible rooms inside a block go first.

Can families pay for their own rooms?

Yes. Each family can pay for its own room.

Do you charge a planning fee?

No.
